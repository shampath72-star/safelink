SafeLink

Press HELP on your phone → laptop sounds a loud alarm so someone can come help.

Install SafeLink on your phone for quick access
Which device is this?
  1. Open this page on your laptop and choose “laptop”. It shows a QR code + 6-digit code.
  2. Scan the QR with any phone camera (or type the code). Enter your name.
  3. You can link many phones to the same laptop.
  4. On the laptop press Arm alarm and keep that tab open.
  5. When someone needs help, they press the big red HELP button.

Works from any browser — no special login needed.
This is a backup only. In danger call 112 (emergency) or 1091 (women helpline) first.
