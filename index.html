SafeLink

Press HELP on your phone → laptop sounds a loud alarm so someone can come help.

Install on your phone
Which device is this?
  1. Open on your laptop → choose “laptop”. QR + code appear.
  2. Scan QR with any phone (or type code) → enter your name.
  3. Link many phones to one laptop.
  4. On laptop press Arm alarm and keep tab open.
  5. When help is needed → press the big red HELP button.

Backup only. In danger call 112 or 1091 first.
